M.S. in Informatics / Researcher / Engineer

Overview

I am Nozomu Yoshida, a Customer Engineer at Google Cloud (AI & Application Modernization) based in Tokyo, partnering with enterprise organizations from technical discovery and PoCs through production architecture across Generative AI, AI Agents, and cloud modernization. Previously, I worked as an R&D Engineer (Part-Time) at Pixie Dust Technologies, Inc. and a Product Manager Intern at Rakuten Group, Inc. (All views expressed on this site are solely my own and do not represent those of my employer.)

My background bridges software engineering and empirical research, grounded in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), learning engineering, and accessibility.

Enterprise AI & Cloud Architecture: Led pre-sales technical engagement for a 2,000+ seat enterprise rollout of an AI coding-assistance platform, co-developed a multimodal video search solution ("ClipSearch", presented at Google Cloud Next Tokyo 2025), and delivered 20+ hands-on customer workshops per year.
  2. Product & Software Engineering: Built Iwate University's first cross-platform (iOS/Android) official news app — adopted by 1,500+ students and staff and ranked 8th in the Productivity category on Google Play in Japan — and drove accessibility product discovery at Rakuten Group.

  4. Honors & Grants: Recipient of the University of Tsukuba Esaki Prize (top 0.7% of graduates) and a JST CREST AIP Challenge research grant.
