Auditory-Centered Vocal Feedback System Using Solmization for Training Absolute Pitch without GUI

HCI Research | Jun 2020 – Sep 2021 | First Author (INTERACT 2021) | Five members

Conventional absolute-pitch training tools rely primarily on visual displays such as tuners or piano-roll guides, even though listeners with absolute pitch typically perceive musical notes verbally as pitch syllables rather than visual symbols. We designed a GUI-free, auditory-centered training system that delivers real-time vocal solmization feedback as notes are played. In a controlled study with 10 participants without absolute pitch, vocal-solmization training improved pitch-identification accuracy by approximately 25% in six participants, whereas a baseline visual-feedback condition yielded no measurable gain.

ElectroMagnetic Guitar: Chord Playing Support System on Guitar by Electromagnets

HCI Research | Apr 2019 – Mar 2020 | First Author (IWAIT 2020, Best Paper Candidate) | Two members

Forming multi-finger chord shapes on a guitar fretboard presents a steep physical barrier for novice players. We developed ElectroMagnetic Guitar (EMG), retrofitting a classical guitar with a custom fretboard of individually addressable electromagnets that guide magnetized fingertip attachments toward target fret positions. Preliminary user evaluations demonstrated sufficient magnetic force to stabilize chord hand postures while clarifying force-density requirements for active finger guidance.

A Picking Interface Seamlessly Connecting Passive and Active Performance on Guitar

HCI Research | Apr 2019 – Mar 2020 | First Author (IWAIT 2020) | Two members

Controlling live visual effects or audio parameters during guitar performance typically requires foot pedals or auxiliary controllers that interrupt natural playing posture. We engineered a sensor-augmented guitar pick that classifies three playing states — chord strumming, single-note picking, and non-picking — in real time from grip pressure and index-finger rotation angle, enabling performers to trigger multimedia effects seamlessly within their natural picking motion with high classification accuracy.
